You Belong at the Table

The Regional Food Sovereignty Council (RFSC) brings Tribal representatives, Elders, youth, and community members together to guide this work — grounded in Unangan knowledge and community voice.

What Is the RFSC?

A council of Tribal representatives, Elders, youth, and community leaders working together to define what food sovereignty and wellbeing mean for this region — and to guide the programs that come from it.

The RFSC plays a central role across all three aims of this project: co-creating the Food Sovereignty and Wellbeing Conceptual Framework, choosing which measures and indicators belong in a regional research toolkit, and guiding the St. Paul pilot program. Members meet 10–12 times over the life of the project (about 3–4 times a year), in person or online.

Why Join?

Being part of the Council benefits you personally, strengthens your community's voice, and builds something lasting for the whole region.

For You

  • Share your knowledge and help shape decisions that matter to your family
  • Build connections with Elders, youth, and leaders region-wide
  • Gain skills in community-led research
  • Compensation provided for your time and travel

For Your Community

  • Make sure local needs and priorities are heard at the regional level
  • Bring back resources, tools, and funding opportunities
  • Strengthen local traditional food programs already underway
  • Support youth–Elder knowledge sharing

For the Region

  • Build a lasting toolkit connecting food sovereignty to wellbeing, created by and for our communities
  • Strengthen partnerships across all 13 Tribes and 10 communities
  • Lay the groundwork for future funding and long-term food security

How the Council Decides

The Council uses the Nominal Group Technique — a structured process that ensures every voice counts equally, so no single perspective dominates the room.

1

Silent Idea Generation

Each person thinks and writes independently before anyone speaks.

2

Round-Robin Sharing

Ideas are shared one at a time, going around the group, without debate.

3

Group Discussion

The group discusses each idea to make sure everyone understands it.

4

Individual Ranking

Each person ranks the ideas privately, and the group's true priorities emerge.
